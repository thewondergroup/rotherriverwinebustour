1
Oxney Estate
Beckley, near Rye

Your day begins at Oxney, the largest single-estate producer of organic wine in England. 35 acres of vines surrounding a Grade II listed oast house winery. You'll enjoy a guided tasting of four of their wines.

4 guided wine tastings
2
Sedlescombe
Near Battle

Next is Sedlescombe, the oldest organic vineyard in England. Here you'll sit down for a Sussex cheese and charcuterie lunch with wine pairings. Vegetarian and vegan options available.

4 wine tastings + lunch with wine pairings
3
Tillingham
Peasmarsh

Your final stop is Tillingham, a historic estate farmed since the 13th century, renowned for its natural wines from 21 different grape varieties. You'll have your final tasting of four wines before heading back to the station.

4 guided wine tastings

Buy wine, we'll ship it home

Buy six or more bottles at any single vineyard and they'll ship them home for free. Carry your bottles on the bus between stops, and when you hit six, hand them over. No lugging bottles on the train.
