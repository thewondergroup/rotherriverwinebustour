The vineyards

Six estates. Six stories.

Each vineyard in the Rother Valley is independently run, with its own character, wines, and welcome.

Oxney Estate
Beckley, near Rye
England's largest single-estate organic vineyard. 35 acres of vines, a Grade II listed oast house winery, and shepherd's hut accommodation among the vines.
Oastbrook Estate
Bodiam, near Robertsbridge
A family-run vineyard renowned for its beautiful setting and exquisite wines. Stay in the Hobbit House, the Avalon Waterside Lodge, or a bell tent among the vines.
Tillingham
Peasmarsh
A historic estate farmed since the 13th century, now making natural wines from 21 grape varieties. Restaurant, 11 bedrooms in a former hop barn, and bell tents in summer.
Charles Palmer
Near Hastings
Home to some of the oldest vines in the Rother Valley, known for a warm welcome, excellent wines, and stunning views stretching to the sea.
Sedlescombe
Near Battle
England's oldest organic vineyard. Decades of experience and craft producing wines with character and depth.
Mountfield
Near Robertsbridge
A stunning vineyard set against a beautiful country estate, making award-winning wines in one of the finest settings in Sussex.
