The vineyards

Six estates. Six stories.

Oxney Estate
Beckley, near Rye
England's largest single-estate organic vineyard. 35 acres of vines, a Grade II listed oast house winery, and shepherd's hut accommodation.
Oastbrook Estate
Bodiam, near Robertsbridge
A family-run vineyard renowned for its beautiful setting and exquisite wines. Stay in the Hobbit House or the Avalon Waterside Lodge.
Tillingham
Peasmarsh
A historic estate farmed since the 13th century, making natural wines from 21 grape varieties. Restaurant, rooms, and bell tents.
Charles Palmer
Near Hastings
Home to some of the oldest vines in the Rother Valley, with a warm welcome and stunning views to the sea.
Sedlescombe
Near Battle
England's oldest organic vineyard. Decades of craft producing wines with character and depth.
Mountfield
Near Robertsbridge
A stunning vineyard set against a beautiful country estate, making award-winning wines.

Love the wine? We'll ship it home.

Buy six or more bottles at any vineyard on the tour and they'll ship them home for free. No lugging bottles on the train.

Questions

Good to know

What happens if it rains?▾
The tours run rain or shine. Tastings happen indoors at each vineyard, and the bus is air-conditioned. You might want to bring a jacket for vineyard walks, but a bit of English weather has never stopped anyone enjoying English wine.
Do I need to know about wine?▾
Not at all. The tastings are guided by the winemakers themselves, and they're used to welcoming everyone from complete beginners to serious collectors. Just come with curiosity.
Can you cater for dietary requirements?▾
Yes. Vegetarian and vegan options are available for the lunch. Please let us know any dietary requirements when you book and we'll make sure everything is sorted.
Can I drive instead of taking the train?▾
The bus picks up and drops off at the station, so the train is the easiest option and means you can enjoy all 12 tastings. If you do drive, you can arrange to meet the bus at the first vineyard, but please contact us in advance.
How many people are on each tour?▾
Tours are kept to a comfortable group size so you get a personal experience at each vineyard. It's a great day for couples, friends, or groups.
Can I buy wine on the tour?▾
Absolutely. You can buy bottles at every vineyard you visit and carry them on the bus. If you buy six or more bottles at any single vineyard, they'll ship them all home for free.
Can I book for a group?▾
Yes, it's a great day out for groups. Just book individual tickets for each person through the booking page. For larger groups or private bookings, please get in touch directly.
Which train do I need to catch?▾
Most tours depart from Robertsbridge Station. Catch the train from London Charing Cross or London Bridge to Hastings, arriving at Robertsbridge at 11:05. The return train is at 16:08. One tour route departs from Rye Station instead, arriving at 10:46 with a 15:48 return.
